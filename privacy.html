Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NkoAha ("NkoAha", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our document management and electronic signature platform at nkoaha.space and app.nkoaha.space (the "Service"). We are committed to handling your data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the NDPR.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), and — where relevant — your organisation name and role.

Documents and content

We store the documents you upload or create, along with annotations, fields, comments, and the signatures collected on them. This content is yours; we process it only to provide the Service.

Signature and audit data

For each signature we record the signer's name and email, the time of signing, and technical metadata such as IP address and device/browser information, so that signed documents carry a verifiable audit trail.

Usage, device and security data

We log security-relevant events — sign-ins, document sends, signatures, payments, and deletions — together with the IP address and device information associated with them. This helps us keep accounts secure and detect abuse.

Payment information

Subscription payments are handled by our payment processors (Flutterwave and Paystack). We do not store full card numbers on our servers; we receive only confirmation of payment and limited transaction details.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
  • To create and preserve the audit trail that supports your signed documents;
  • To authenticate you and keep your account and documents secure;
  • To process subscriptions and send service-related emails (sign requests, confirmations, password resets);
  • To respond to your support requests;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the basis of: performance of our contract with you (to deliver the Service); your consent (where required, e.g. optional communications); our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, improving the Service); and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Your documents and signatures

Your documents belong to you. We access their contents only as needed to operate the Service (for example, to render pages, route signatures, or at your request). We do not sell your documents or use their contents for advertising. If you use our AI drafting feature ("Uburu AI"), the text you submit is sent to the configured AI provider solely to generate your draft and is not used by us to train models.

5. Who we share information with

We share data only with service providers who help us run the platform, under appropriate data-processing terms:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage hosting;
  • Resend — transactional email delivery;
  • Flutterwave and Paystack — payment processing;
  • AI providers (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI) — only where you actively use AI drafting, limited to the text you submit;
  • Other signers on a document — who necessarily see the document shared with them.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of NkoAha and its users.

6. How we protect your information

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your documents is enforced at the database level with row-level security so that only you and the people you authorise can reach them. We log security events and support blocking abusive IP addresses. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data using industry-standard measures.

7. Data retention

We keep your account and document data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a document it is removed from active storage; signed documents and their audit records may be retained where needed to preserve the integrity of completed agreements or to meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account at any time.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct inaccurate data, to request deletion, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@nkoaha.space.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser storage only for essential purposes — keeping you signed in and remembering basic preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

10. International transfers

Some of our service providers process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we rely on providers that offer appropriate safeguards for the protection of your data.

11. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you through the Service.
