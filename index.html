What NkoAha does

Everything your documents need.
Nothing they don't.

Purpose-built for African business realities — built to handle the documents that move your work forward.

E-Signatures that hold up

Collect legally binding signatures from anyone — clients, vendors, staff — without them needing an account. They click, sign, done.

CORE

Document Management

Upload PDFs and Word docs. Organise your business files in one place. No more searching through email attachments.

Your Document Drive

Every document you create, upload, or sign lives in your secure cloud drive. Search, sort, and pull up any file in seconds — originals and signed copies, always backed up.

Signature Routing

Set the order: Party A signs, then Party B gets notified. Documents route automatically in the sequence you define.

Overlay Annotations

Add text boxes, dates, tables, and images on top of any document. Place elements exactly where they need to go, then stamp it done.

Team & Organisation

Add team members, control who sees what, and manage document flows across your whole organisation — with role-based permissions.

Comment & Collaborate

Leave comments directly on documents. Discuss changes, flag issues, and track conversations — all in context, without back-and-forth emails.

The process

From upload to signed in minutes.

Four steps. No training required. Works on any device.

1

Upload your document

Upload any PDF or Word document. NkoAha converts it automatically, ready for annotation and signing.

2

Place signature fields

Drag signature boxes, dates, and text fields exactly where you need them. Set who signs in what order.

3

Send to signers

Signers receive an email with a direct link. They open, sign, and submit — no account needed on their end.

4

Download the signed copy

Once all parties sign, everyone gets a final copy automatically. Your original stays safe in your document drive.
